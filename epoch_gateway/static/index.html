Cognitive twins of human experts.

Give EPOCH an expert's raw material — audio, video, documents, images from Dr. Sarah Chen's clinical work. It learns how she reasons and answers new questions the way she would, showing you which of her traces it drew on.

Who you are — and who answers

You're a clinician inside one hospital — a doctor, resident, or radiographer your hospital issued a token to. Not a patient. You have a chest film and the radiologist isn't around: 2 a.m. in the ED, a clinic with no radiologist on site, a resident learning to read films. Instead of a hallway curbside consult you ask Dr. Chen's cognitive twin — a stand-in for her read, built from her documented reasoning. Not her, live.

  1. YOUupload a film and a question. Your token already carries your role and your hospital.
  2. GATEWAYchecks your identity and binds the request to your hospital. A hospital-A token used as hospital B is refused.
  3. TRACE LAYERretrieves the closest patterns from Dr. Chen's corpus — your hospital's copy only — and follows their links one hop.
  4. TWIN LAYERasks Vertex Gemini to write the read as Dr. Chen, grounded on exactly those patterns and the pixels of your film.
  5. YOUget her twin's answer and the traces it leaned on — click one to read the source.

Your token carries two facts. hospital (tenant_A / tenant_B) — each hospital holds its own isolated copy of Dr. Chen's knowledge; nothing crosses between them. role (admin / viewer) — admin can trigger privileged actions, viewer can only ask. In this demo you choose both with Get a demo token; in production your hospital's SSO sets them and you never see the token.

How Dr. Chen's twin got here

Dr. Sarah Chen, a thoracic radiologist, is the worked example the whole of Phase 5 is built around — each session moved her twin one step closer to this URL.

  • 15.1Indexed. Her corpus — chest films, reports, dictations — loaded into a FAISS index fenced to her hospital by row-level security.
  • 15.2–15.3Bound. Her reasoning wrapped in a role-aware agent: only the tools her role allows, a per-role model, an audit trail, and an 8-second deadline on every answer.
  • 16.1–16.2Served. That agent went behind the gateway as /v1 and /v2/agent/invoke; the smoke suite checks that her role and her citations survive the round trip.
  • 17.1–17.3Shipped. Hardened into an image, given a private registry and a scoped identity, and deployed here — her twin now answers from a public address.

On this deployment: Dr. Chen's corpus ships inside the container as ~20 diagnostic traces per site. On boot each site's traces are embedded (Vertex text-embedding-004) into its own FAISS index. A question retrieves the top matches from your site only, expands one hop along their pattern links, and the Vertex Gemini read is grounded on exactly those traces. The cited IDs are the traces the twin actually drew on — click one to read it. The whole pipeline runs under the 15.3 hard deadline.

  • The corpus is her reasoning, not a case archive. Each trace is a pattern — consolidation, effusion, tension pneumothorax — with the features she keys on and her logic. The film you upload is treated as new; nothing in the corpus is a prior read of it.
  • Retrieval is driven by your text, not the image. Your words are matched against the pattern library; the film's pixels only reach the generation step. "Here's a film, what do you see?" gives retrieval little to grab — describe what you're looking at and it works.
  • The corpus was written, not extracted. The spec's pipeline learns the patterns from her raw dictations and annotated cases. Dr. Chen is a course persona with no dictations, so the ~20 patterns per site were authored directly. Everything on top — embedding, retrieval, one-hop expansion, grounding, citations — is real.

The build — Phase 5, session by session

  • 15.1Trace Layer. Per-tenant FAISS, Postgres row-level security, isolated checkpointing.
  • 15.2Action isolation. JWT auth, a role → tools RBAC matrix, per-role model binding, an audit log.
  • 15.3The SLA. A hard per-query deadline — a timeout becomes a 504 — plus a circuit breaker.
  • 16.1The cluster. The gateway wired to the LLM backend.
  • 16.2Smoke suite. Eight integration tests, trace-span assertions over status codes, /v1 + /v2 contracts.
  • 16.xLoad & metering. A Locust load profile and per-query usage metering.
  • 17.1The image. Packaged, non-root, vulnerability-scanned.
  • 17.2GCP foundation. Artifact Registry and a least-privilege runtime service account.
  • 17.3This deploy. Public Cloud Run, Secret Manager, a private VPC path, and this UI.
